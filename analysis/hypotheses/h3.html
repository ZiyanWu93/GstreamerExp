H3 - Media Workload Calibration

Claim: media workload calibration gates realistic 5G trace comparisons.

Current verdict: supported

The current ball workload under-drives all three translated 5G traces. That means the old trace comparisons should not be used as SCReAM-vs-GCC evidence: the experiment was mostly application-limited, not network-limited.

Why This Replaces the Old Trace Hypotheses

A SCReAM-vs-GCC comparison is meaningful only if the video stream puts pressure on the shaped path. The previous trace runs used ball-720p30-30s, which encoded to roughly 220-230 kbit/s even when the encoder target was higher. The CQI, handover-like, and resource-block traces usually provide several Mbit/s, so both controllers had enough capacity most of the time.

Decision Rule

The workload is considered under-driving if every translated trace has at least 10x median-capacity headroom over observed camera wire bitrate, and fewer than 15% of 100 ms bins below 1 Mbit/s. Passing this rule is a failure of the workload for algorithm comparison, even though it is a successful calibration diagnosis.

Findings and Limitations

  • Finding: the old ball workload is too compressible to stress these translated traces most of the time.
  • Finding: the previous realistic-trace SCReAM/GCC near-ties are not meaningful algorithm evidence.
  • Next experiment: use high-quality or high-motion video and verify actual emitted bitrate before making SCReAM/GCC claims.
